Game Music

Generative ambient music made in the browser, with no sound files. This page drives the library exactly as a game would: every control sends one of the messages shown in the log at the bottom.

Headphones help. The low drone and the heartbeat are lost on small speakers. Press Start, wait half a minute, then change the mood.
